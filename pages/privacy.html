Privacy Policy

This explains what personal data the Drix Tech Talent Programme collects, why, and how it's protected — in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023.

1. Who We Are

The Drix Tech Talent Programme ("Programme", "we", "us") is a free tech fellowship operated by Drix Software Foundation. This policy applies to anyone who registers on, or uses, the Drix Tech Talent Hub platform.

2. What We Collect

When you register and use the platform, we collect:

  • Identity and contact information: full name, email address, phone number, gender, date of birth
  • Location information: country and state/region of residence
  • Programme records: your track, cohort, and — where applicable — your official 3MTT ID
  • Learning activity: lesson progress, assignment submissions and grades, weekly check-ins, points and badges
  • Account credentials: your password, stored only as an irreversible cryptographic hash (bcrypt) — we never store or can see your actual password
  • Content you choose to make public: your portfolio, if and when you turn it on

3. Why We Collect It

We use your data strictly to run the Programme: to verify eligibility, track your learning progress, connect you with a mentor, communicate updates, issue certificates, and — where you've been selected into a partner programme such as 3MTT — to report your participation and progress to that programme's administrators.

We do not use your data for advertising targeting, and any advertising shown on our public marketing pages is not based on your personal or learning data.

4. Data We Do Not Sell

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Your data is not shared outside the Programme except:

  • With your assigned mentor, limited to what's needed for them to support your learning
  • With partner programmes (such as 3MTT) you have been selected into, limited to programme-relevant progress data
  • With service providers who operate the platform on our behalf (hosting, database, email delivery), bound to only use your data to provide that service
  • Where required by law or a valid legal process

5. How We Protect Your Data

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt — never stored or transmitted in plain text
  • All API keys and credentials are stored server-side only and are never exposed to the browser
  • Portfolio links use a fellow-chosen slug, not a predictable database ID, so your record can't be scraped by guessing sequential links
  • Password resets use a time-limited, single-use one-time code, never a reusable link
  • Access to fellow data on the admin side is role-restricted to authorised programme staff

6. Your Rights Under the NDPA

As a data subject under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal data we hold about you and request a copy of it
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Ask us to delete your data, subject to any legal or programme-reporting obligations that require us to retain certain records
  • Withdraw consent for optional uses of your data (such as email notifications or a public portfolio) at any time from your account settings
  • Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) if you believe your data has been mishandled

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward to satisfy programme reporting, certification, and legal record-keeping obligations. You may request earlier deletion, subject to those obligations.

8. Contact Us

For any questions about this policy or your data, reach us through the Contact page.
